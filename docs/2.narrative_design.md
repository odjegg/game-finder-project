David is a manager for several prominent twitch streamers that play a lot of games for their audience. He has decided that it is a good idea for streamers to keep track of the many titles they’ve played. As part of this, he has asked them to write down the names of each game they’ve played so that they don’t play the same thing twice. 

Currently, the streamers he manages are using https://rawg.io/ as a database to archive each title. However, David also needs a social media element so streamers can communicate on if a game is worth playing and therefore if it would be worthwhile playing or not. He also needs a way to see the exact platform they’ve played on. For example, they might have played some games on a “PlayStation”, and the console generation needs to be clarified instead of just showing the “PlayStation” logo. This would also be beneficial as certain games might not perform as well on certain platforms. 

He needs to be able to keep track of games that are released quarterly and sort by rating so that he can recommend up-and-coming or trendy games to his employees. He would also need the ability to sort by various other metrics, as some of his clients may not intrinsically want to stream trendy games. 

David reaches out to other managers for any software recommendations. One replies and recommends “Backloggd”, a game logging site that includes ratings, play status and a social element but lacks the correct tools for team communication, collaboration and relevant managerial tracking features. Another manager suggests using a platform such as “Discord” or “Microsoft Teams” to solve the lack of detailed tracking, communication or collaboration tools. However, David finds it difficult to find a platform that facilitates his exact requirements.

# Scenario 

Finding the Next Game — Liam Carter 

Liam Carter is a 19 year old upcoming streamer. He plays and streams games online from which he makes most of his money. People find his gameplay in Rainbow Six Siege and Call of Duty quite interesting and he has a lot of viewers. He constantly needs to find new and exciting games to keep his audience engaged and grow his channel. As his channel grows, the pressure to consistently deliver fresh and entertaining content becomes increasingly demanding. 

However, Liam finds it increasingly difficult and time consuming to discover new titles using platforms such as GameVault, often spending hours scrolling through hundreds of games with no way to filter by genre or rating. This wasted time was cutting into his streaming schedule and affecting the quality of his content. One evening after streaming, he came across an advertisement for GameFinder on social media. He was intrigued by its idea and its helpful features. He proceeded to download it on his PC and was instantly pleased by it and found its features useful. 

Using GameFinder's filter feature, Liam was able to narrow down games by genre, platform and rating within minutes. He searched specifically for first person shooter games similar to Call of Duty, discovering several hidden titles his audience had never seen streamed before. He streamed one of the recommended games the following night to a great response from his viewers, gaining new followers in the process. GameFinder quickly became an essential tool in planning his streaming content, saving him hours of unnecessary scrolling and allowing him to focus on what he does best — entertaining his audience.

## Name: Ethan Walker

## Age: 17

## Occupation: Sixth Form Student  

As a casual gamer, I want to see up to date ratings and reviews for each game, so that I can make informed decisions about which games are worth buying.Currently many game discovery platforms display outdated ratings that do not reflect a game's current reputation or quality. This has led me to waste money purchasing games that turned out to be poorly received by the gaming community. GameFinder's regularly updated database sourced from RAWG would ensure that all ratings and reviews are current and accurate. This would give me confidence that the information I am basing my purchasing decisions on is reliable and trustworthy.
